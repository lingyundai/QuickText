Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you text QuickText, we collect your phone number and the content of your messages. We may also store your location information if you provide it to improve search results.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to respond to your requests via SMS. Specifically:

— To process your food search queries and return relevant results.
— To remember your location for faster future searches.
— To send SMS responses to your inquiries.

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond delivering the QuickText service.

3. SMS Messaging

By texting QuickText at (866) 945-5744, you consent to receive SMS messages in direct response to your requests. QuickText is an on-demand restaurant recommendation service — messages are only sent in reply to your queries. No unsolicited or marketing messages are ever sent.

Message frequency varies based on your use. Msg & Data rates may apply.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message. To request help, reply HELP.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. We may share data only as required to operate the service (for example, passing your search query to Google Places API to retrieve restaurant results).

5. Data Retention

We retain your phone number and location preference to improve your experience. You may request deletion of your data at any time by texting STOP.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of QuickText after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
